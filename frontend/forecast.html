03 / METHODS & LIMITS

What the chart can—and cannot—say

Baseline: Ordinary least squares on up to eight latest available annual MAP estimates. Nonnegative forecasts are enforced; proportion indicators are also capped at 100%. Historical MAP values are themselves modelled estimates, not raw case counts.

Backtest: Train on earlier available years and predict the final three historical years. MAE is reported in the original indicator units. It is not a calibrated future uncertainty interval.

Scenario: The selected reduction is a hypothetical, constant percentage change to the projected outcome from the first forecast year. It is not a causal impact estimate, clinical recommendation, budget optimisation, or validated epidemiological forecast.

Data: Reads the existing frontend/data/frontend-data.json snapshot locally; no API or backend is required. No changes to source data.
